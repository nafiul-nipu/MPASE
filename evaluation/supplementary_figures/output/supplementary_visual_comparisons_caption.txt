Supplementary Fig. X. Side-by-side visual comparisons of representative chromosome shapes corresponding to the quantitative usage-case results. Each block shows chromosome-level HDR shapes in the YZ projection at the 100% abstraction level for UNTR and VACV conditions across 12h, 18h, and 24h. The selected chromosomes illustrate a range of structural behaviors, including relatively stable patterns and stronger infection-associated changes. These visual comparisons complement the IoU and meanNN values reported in the supplementary table by showing the shape differences underlying the quantitative trends.
